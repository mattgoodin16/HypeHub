About HypeHub Supply

Built for students, resellers, and anyone who loves premium gear without premium prices.

The idea

HypeHub Supply started as a campus side hustle: finding ways to source legit gear at wholesale-level prices and passing those savings to friends. The project evolved into a small, curated storefront focused on the intersection of hype culture and practicality.

What we offer

  • Iconic pieces from brands like Polo, Supreme, Fear of God Essentials, ASICS, Denim Tears, and more.
  • Student-friendly pricing and a Buy 2, get 10% off promo that runs across the entire catalog.
  • A clean, minimal shopping experience that works seamlessly on desktop and mobile.

Important note

This site is a mock concept used to demonstrate an e‑commerce layout and front‑end cart experience. It is not processing real payments or fulfilling real orders.

Quick facts

  • • Front‑end only (no backend required).
  • • Static files, perfect for GitHub + Vercel deployment.
  • • Client‑side cart with local storage.
